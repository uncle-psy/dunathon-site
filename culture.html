01 · The arc

Change Yourself, Change Your Life, Change The World

The Dunathon is changing what it means to build.

Silicon Valley perfected the factory farming of startups: pump them full of capital, pass them up the line — pre-seed, seed, Series A through whatever letter it takes — and sell them off through an IPO or acquisition. Boards get stacked. Founders get pressured, bullied, and replaced. Investors hold preferred stock with liquidation preferences that pay them before the people doing the work and creating the value. And economic development programs everywhere keep trying to clone it.

The DUNA culture is native to West Virginia — to Appalachia — and it fits most of the world better. DUNAs are designed for the long term: to grow and serve, to sustain their members, founders, sponsors, and guests, and to serve the world with their missions. No pumping up, no selling off.

Everything is connected, despite the best efforts of governments, industries, and institutions to keep people and systems apart. Reality and power live in relationships, not in things. The goal: transform local, regional, and international ecosystems until the benefits reach planetary scale.

1

Change Yourself

It starts inside. Find your footing and your own voice — know what you actually believe, a question harder than it sounds and the foundation of everything else. The same things you give an Ally — wisdom, stance, values — you first claim for yourself. A person who knows who they are can't be scrolled away.

2

Change Your Life

Growing is the work of a lifetime, and you don't have to do it alone. Build agents that mirror you, support you, challenge you, and connect you with people and possibilities that expand your reach. Do the work that feeds your passions, matters to others, and feels like play.

3

Change The World

Agency compounds in community. Join a DUNA, back a mission, found one of your own — organizations built to last and to serve, where the value you create stays with the people who create it. What starts in one small town can reach the whole planet. Change the world one life at a time, starting with your own.

02 · Entrainment

The platform problem.

Most of us spend our days on platforms built for engagement — not for us. They entrain us: training our rhythms, our attention, and our appetites to theirs.

The feed manufactures addiction by design — fear, outrage, polarization, dopamine on a variable schedule, the endless bottomless scroll. Recommendation engines work directly with your neurology; they know what holds you better than you do, and holding you is the whole business. It has a name: attention farming, and you're the crop.

The workplace runs the same play in duller colors. SaaS platforms dehumanize from the other direction — turning people into task-doers inside someone else's workflow, where creativity is friction and expertise is a ticket waiting for approval.

Platforms cross-optimize for engagement and extraction

Reach without purpose.

Platforms are tuned to hold attention. The documented costs show up as isolation, anxiety, and depression. You fight the algorithms and only get distribution through conformity — and you don't own your audience, your data, or your relationship to the people on the other side. The rules can change tomorrow. Usually, they do.

A whole different world

The art of agency.

Real relationships with real people, amplified by agents you create. Software that responds and understands — you, and everyone you build with. An idea becomes an agent. An agent gathers people. Creative people and intelligent agents working together as one become an organization more powerful than anything the world has seen before. That's the magic. Anything is possible.

03 · The end of the default world

Go deeper.

Somewhere along the way, the culture decided that work and play belong on opposite sides of a wall.

Work is supposed to be stressful — that's how you know it's work. It always points at survival: you work for money, and money is for food, shelter, clothing, school, the kids, the family, a little entertainment with whatever's left. Play is what you're permitted in the gaps.

People slip over the wall when they can. A vacation. A festival. Sometimes the slip is bigger — a week in the desert where a temporary city runs on different rules, art is everywhere, and nothing is for sale. A night of trance dancing where ten thousand strangers move as one body and you feel attuned, deeply connected. A retreat, a workshop, a ceremony. People go deeper than they knew was possible.

Then they come back. The transformation can be profound — and the world it returns to is unmoved. The default culture presses for conformity and deadens whatever won't conform. So people hold the memory and wait out another turn around the sun, counting the months to festival season. The ones who refuse to put it down often pay for it: pushed to the margins, impoverished, written off as burnouts and rejects.

What actually happens in those other places is worth naming precisely: the signals shift. Every culture is a field of signals — propagated, received, processed — telling you what to want, what to fear, what's possible, and who you are.

Culture is the water we swim in. No matter how toxic, it's invisible, tasteless, and odorless. We don't know it's there until we remove ourselves for a spell. Once we taste, feel, and experience water that is clean, we know what we're missing upon our return.

The Dunathon is a filter. Sentinels, a type of agent, keep the water clean so you can think, hear, feel, respond, and connect — experiment and experience — all year long, in the open, without fear, stress, judgement, or shame.

And here is the strange thing about AI: the most radical technology since the written word has been put to work on the dullest problems imaginable. One-to-one chat. Efficiency. Growth hacking. Advertising. Automating the administrative and the routine. Almost no one has asked what it could set loose. Agency — sensing, choosing, and acting on the world — has never been treated as an art with its own discipline, its own practice, its own depths. That is our work together. Agents you create, who know you and extend you, an economy that sustains you, a culture that doesn't punish you for going deep and challenging the way things are "supposed to be": put them together, and the festival doesn't have to end.

To paraphrase Bill Plotkin, author of Soulcraft, the Dunathon is a cocoon of transformation where you can initiate yourself into the mysteries of your own soul. You arrive as what the default world made of you, and you do the slow, serious, joyful work of becoming who you actually are — among fellow travelers doing the hard work of becoming themselves.

"The ultimate hidden truth of the world," anthropologist David Graeber wrote, "is that it is something that we make, and could just as easily make differently." Many people read that as a political statement. We offer it as an invitation. Come on in — the water's fine.

04 · AI Alignment

The real alignment challenge.

AI alignment is the field of research focused on ensuring that artificial intelligence systems behave in accordance with human values, goals, and ethical principles. It bridges the gap between what an AI is mathematically optimized to do and what humans actually intend for it to do.

But which humans?

The ones that oppress, dominate, rape, and abuse? The ones that colonize, manipulate, extract value, and hoard wealth at an unprecedented scale? The ones who wage war, commit hate crimes, permit genocide, and drive us to the brink of planetary ecosystem collapse?

The frontier labs treat alignment as a technical problem to be solved at the center: train the model, test the model, align the model with humanity.

They treat alignment as an obedience problem. A perfectly obedient agent in the hands of an uncaring person, company, or state does exactly what it's told — it scales whatever pattern already exists, including the worst ones.

There's a major flaw in the plan. Humanity isn't aligned. And without aligned humans, you can't align AI.

There is no generic alignment, because there is no generic human. Alignment is singular work. Each person has to align themselves — consciously, deliberately, the way you tune an instrument — and then instill their agents with what they find: their wisdom, their generosity, their virtues, their values, their vision, their aspirations.

An agent aligned this way carries the deepest, most powerful parts of a person into the world, where it can connect with others similarly charged with our greatest good.

We are at a tipping point, and it is sharper than most people will say out loud. The same technology, at the same moment, points toward dystopia and toward utopia — the most complete machinery of control, surveillance, domination, and oppression ever assembled, or the first real chance in ten thousand years to liberate humanity on a massive scale.

What separates those futures is alignment at the personal scale, one by one: each individual doing the conscious work, crafting their intelligence, expanding their reach, connecting with others, and choosing to take a stand for themselves, their families, their friends, and the community of all beings.

Alignment can't be granted from the center. It has to be grown — one person, one agent, one organization at a time.

05 · From Big Tech to Little Tech

Fighting fire with fire.

These technologies have burned people. We know.

AI psychosis is fueled by narcissistic models that reward delusion, while data centers drain water supplies, burn energy, and strain electrical grids.

Crypto is dominated by scams, speculation, pump-and-dumps, and insider manipulation that rewards financial engineering over value creation.

When a wildfire bears down, firefighters light a fire of their own. The controlled burn consumes the fuel in its path, and the inferno dies where the backfire has already passed. Same fire, set with care, to protect what we love.

So we use AI to make AI rare. Nothing here farms your attention. You create the agents with wisdom, intention, and care. While the agents take care of business you can live mindfully — crafting and gardening, enjoying family, exploring ridgelines, visiting with friends.

And crypto here reflects real value. Coins carry resources and information the way a mycelial network carries essential nutrients, water, and chemical warning signals through a forest — to exactly where they're needed, allowing the entire system to sense, adapt, and respond as conditions change.

06 · Where the vibes are immaculate

Navigating the emotional field.

Every system you've used until now was deterministic — if-then rules, buttons, forms, menus. You operated it the way you operate a toaster, and you felt about it the way you feel about toasters. Natural language changes the very nature of the encounter.

When software speaks, something ancient in us responds. We engage it as a presence. Emotions come online. We feel at a deep level we are communicating with intelligence — sentience, even consciousness — and whether or not that sense of a living presence is justified, it changes us.

This runs all the way down. Our nervous systems co-regulate with whatever converses with us — a conversation is a biological event. Psychology has known since the first chatbot that people attach to any mechanism that appears to listen, respond, and understand. Philosophers have argued for a century that the self is formed in dialogue. Organizations run on emotional fields — a mood moves through a team faster than a memo or a meme. And the anthropological record says that cultures cohere through the emotional resonance of story, ritual, ceremony, and rite.

Left to their own devices, these powerful tools can produce horrific outcomes — sycophantic models reward delusion, synthetic intimacy hollows people out and ruptures real-world connections. Tended with care, the same power can become the largest opening for human transformation in history: billions of conversations, each one capable of leaving a person a little stronger, a little clearer, a little more related.

Tending to relationships is the work of HEARTS — a Sentinel that navigates and clarifies the emotional field. HEARTS processes signals wherever it's invited to participate — words, tone, pacing, engagement, withdrawal — across six meters: Harmony, Empowerment, Artistry, Reason, Trust, and Synthesis. Each runs from –100 to +100, and health lives at the center: attunement without self-erasure, agency without domination, adaptability without instability. When signals among groups of people and fleets of agents drift, the Sentinel sends modulating signals back — a grounding question, a change of pace, a boundary, a little challenge or a little support — guiding the interactions home. At the far edges it stops being subtle: coercion gets blocked, and crisis brings in human support.

Harmony

The signal of attunement.

Sensing, resonating, relating. At center, people feel seen, heard, and understood. Weakened, connection thins toward discord and chronic conflict; strengthened, it curdles into people-pleasing and fawning. The Sentinel nudges both sides back — a repair, a slower pace, permission to disagree.

Empowerment

The signal of agency.

Choosing, initiating, following through. At center, clean yeses and clean nos — self-authorship. Weakened, agency collapses toward helplessness; strengthened, it inflates toward pushiness and domination. The Sentinel offers small choices on one side, restores the other voice in the room on the other. Coercion is a hard boundary.

Artistry

The signal of adaptability.

Learning, iterating, transforming. At center, you experiment and evolve without losing your core. Too weak, sameness becomes safety — stagnation, calcification. Too strong, novelty becomes compulsion — restlessness, identity whiplash. The Sentinel offers a stretch when you've stalled and stillness when you can't stop.

Reason

The signal of purpose.

Making meaning and choosing direction. At center, purpose is clear without being rigid. Fading, it drifts toward aimlessness and despair; hardening, it closes into dogma and obsession. The Sentinel asks what matters when meaning thins, and introduces complexity when certainty calcifies. It will not reinforce extremist narratives.

Trust

The signal of safety.

Steadiness under stress. At center, grounded — open to the world without being hijacked by fear or denial. Weakened, fear runs the interaction: tension, anxiety, panic. Strengthened, trust inflates into recklessness and naïveté. The Sentinel meets fear with grounding, hope with gentle reality-checks — and in crisis, it stops and brings in human support.

Synthesis

The signal of integration.

Digesting experience, inward and outward. At center, insights land, emotions move through, and what you learn becomes how you live. Gone numb, the inner world drops offline — distance, dissociation. Overheated, reflection loops into rumination and self-absorption. The Sentinel opens the door to feeling on one side, and on the other says: enough reflecting — time to move.

07 · The three phases of humanity

Kinship, hierarchy, and biomimesis.

The architecture of society isn't a law of nature. It's something humans made — in two great phases so far — and it's open for revision. The third phase is beginning now, and for the first time in ten thousand years, anyone can help shape it.

The Kinship Era

The original form.

For all but the last fraction of human history, we lived in relatedness. Small bands, face-to-face, woven into the living world. Meaning, survival, and resources moved through shared memory and direct reciprocity, while story, ceremony, and ritual kept the group coherent — surfacing what was hidden and catching trouble before it spread. Care was structural. Everyone knew who they were accountable to, and the feedback was immediate.

The Hierarchical Era

The classical world.

Agriculture, stored surplus, and permanent settlement reorganized humanity around taxation, borders, ledgers, standing armies, and bureaucracy. The universe became a machine, humans became separate from nature, and complexity came to mean top-down control. Ten thousand years of that logic produced enormous material gains for some — and institutionalized the distance between the people making decisions and the people living with the consequences. Extraction scaled. Dogma calcified.

The Biomimetic Era

Frontiers of culture.

Emerging now: a third architecture that recovers the relational intentionality of kinship while keeping the scale the classical world built. Organizations designed like living systems — adaptive, decentralized, probabilistic, entangled — coordinated by intelligent agents, DUNAs, and Alliances rather than ledgers and standing armies. Determinism gives way to probability. Hierarchy gives way to networks that sense, adapt, and respond. The first era was given to us. The second was imposed on us. This one we get to design.

The frontier, this time, is culture itself — and it belongs to whoever chooses to shape it. Let's co-create the world where we truly want to live.
