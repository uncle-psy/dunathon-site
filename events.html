The Dunathon is itself a DUNA — the genesis DUNA — and everyone builds on it. It runs online, worldwide, from July 7 through September 9.

July 7 – September 9 · Online, worldwide
The genesis DUNA, open to everyone.

The Dunathon is both a competition and a collaboration: information and resources are shared. Members research innovations across four tracks — governance, technology, culture, and economics — and decide together, by buying tokens, what the treasury funds. No outside judges. The members decide.

06 · How it works

The Dunathon rules.

How funding and decisions work for the whole summer.

1

Members decide.

All decisions are made by Dunathon members. No outside judges, no organizing committee with a veto. The members run this.

2

Vote by buying.

Group votes distribute resources by buying a proposal's tokens. The more members back an idea, the more funding it draws from the treasury.

3

Everything is double.

Every choice must serve the mission and build the value of the token behind it. Good ideas earn both.

4

You don't need your own DUNA to win.

Back what you believe in and share in the outcome. You can participate fully as a member of the genesis DUNA — no need to spin out.

5

Build in the open.

Information, tools, and resources are shared across DUNAs. This is a collaboration as much as a competition.

6

Spin out when ready.

Take your DUNA independent once you reach 100 members or hit the raise you set. The genesis DUNA stays your registered agent until you do.

7

Keep creating.

You can start new DUNAs any time up to September 9. There is no cap on how many can form — or how far they can go.
