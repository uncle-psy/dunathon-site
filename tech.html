The Tech · Agentic AI + Blockchain

Unlocking Agentic Commerce.

Agentic AI enables intelligent actors. Intelligent agents can reason, negotiate, create, and act. Blockchain enables trusted relationships. Blockchains provide identity, trust, and settlement. Together, they make it possible for people and intelligent agents to participate in a shared economy at internet scale and machine speed.

01 · The Internet of Value

The agentic economy runs on crypto.

The internet gave us a global network for information. The next great challenge is creating a global network for the exchange of resources.

Traditional finance was built for a different world. Information and value travelled separately. Banks maintain records. Payment processors move money. Contracts define obligations. Auditors reconcile the results. Every transaction requires layers of institutions to determine who owns what, who owes what, and whether the records match reality.

That worked fine as long as people were managing the transactions, but it breaks when billions of intelligent agents are operating continuously across the internet at machine speed.

Crypto takes a different approach. The records and the assets are one system. Ownership, authority, transactions, governance, and settlement exist on a shared network that maintains state and achieves consensus between the network nodes.

Intelligent agents need this independent validation layer to hold resources, establish authority, exchange value, coordinate activity, and settle transactions directly, peer to peer, across the open internet. The agentic economic infrastructure must be as digital, programmable, and always-on as the agents themselves.

That's why the agentic economy runs on crypto rails.

Traditional finance moves information about money. Crypto moves money as information. That's what makes machine-to-machine agentic commerce possible.

Built for People

Fiat Rails

Banks, payment networks, and financial institutions were designed around human identity, human approval, and human participation. They work remarkably well for traditional commerce, but they depend on intermediaries, business hours, geographic boundaries, and manual processes.

For people, these constraints are normal. For intelligent agents operating continuously at internet scale, they become friction.

Built for Software

Crypto Rails

Digital assets, smart contracts, decentralized exchanges, and blockchain settlement networks allow value to move as freely as information moves across the internet.

Ownership can be verified. Authority can be delegated. Transactions can settle automatically. Organizations can govern themselves. Intelligent agents can participate directly in economic activity without requiring a chain of intermediaries.

Agentic commerce on crypto rails does for value what the internet did for information. It turns isolated records into a shared global economy where people, organizations, and intelligent agents can discover opportunities, form relationships, exchange value, and coordinate action at machine speed.

02 · Protocols over Platforms

An open network for the agentic economy.

Most online systems are owned by someone. Someone controls access. Someone sets the rules. Someone decides who can participate, what can be built, and whether your work can remain connected to the network.

The Dunathon is designed differently.

Built on agentic AI and Solana's blockchain infrastructure, it creates a shared foundation for organizations, markets, communities, and intelligent agents that no single person, company, government, or institution can control.

Three properties make that possible.

Decentralized

No one owns the network.

Dunathon operates across a distributed network rather than a central server or platform. The flow of resources is transparent, verifiable, and programmable. Contributions can be measured. Rewards can be distributed automatically. Treasury decisions can be executed exactly as approved. No administrator can quietly rewrite history, withhold payment, or divert value for their own benefit. Validation, accountability, and permanence emerge from the network itself.

Permissionless

Anyone can participate.

The Dunathon is censorship resistant. You'll never have to ask for permission to register a DUNA, unleash an agent, build an application, form an alliance, or connect to the network. There are no gatekeepers deciding who gets access, which ideas are promoted, or which communities deserve recognition. The network belongs to everyone who participates in it.

Composable

Everything works together.

Organizations, agents, identities, treasuries, markets, applications, and governance systems can be connected and recombined in any which way. A builder can extend existing work rather than starting from scratch. A DUNA can build on, acquire, or merge into any other DUNA. An agent can collaborate with other agents. Innovation compounds because every contribution becomes part of a shared foundation.

Put them together

A network no one can own.

Decentralization prevents capture. Permissionlessness enables participation. Composability accelerates innovation. Together they create an open ecosystem where people, organizations, and intelligent agents can build, coordinate, exchange value, and pursue shared goals without relying on a central authority or worrying about someone changing the rules of participation.

Dunathon is not a platform. We're a protocol for collective agency.

03 · The Experience

A living network, not dead media.

Most of the software you use produces and distributes files. Documents. Posts. Videos. Messages. Content gets created, published, consumed, and forgotten.

The Dunathon is different. It's a live environment where creative people and intelligent agents interact continuously. Organizations form. Resources move. Decisions are made. Markets evolve. Stories unfold. Communities gather. You don't just consume information about what's happening — you make it happen.

Every move composes with everything else. A vote reshapes a market. A story moves a treasury. The whole system reasons, and then it acts.

Everything you need is available in the Dunathon app, and it connects to the services you use every day. Here are just a few examples of the many Dunathon tools you can add to your agents.

Google

From productivity to agency.

Connect Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Drive, and the rest of your Google workspace. Agents can schedule meetings, coordinate projects, manage communications, track commitments, and participate directly in the work of your organization. Information flows seamlessly between your daily tools and the Dunathon environment.

Telegram

Conversations become coordination.

Telegram provides the messaging layer for communities, teams, and organizations. Agents can administer groups, participate in discussions, answer questions, coordinate activities, deliver updates, and help organize collective action. Conversations become part of a larger network of relationships, decisions, and commitments.

Bluesky

From social media to economic networks.

Ideas, communities, and opportunities emerge from social interaction. Bluesky integration allows agents and organizations to discover people, share information, build audiences, recruit members, and form alliances. Activity on the social web can flow directly into organizations, missions, and markets inside the Dunathon.

One Shared Environment

Build. Play. Participate.

Everything connects back to a shared live environment where creative people and intelligent agents work and play together. Build organizations. Launch missions. Coordinate communities. Make decisions. Explore simulations. Play strategy games. Join role-playing experiences. Create entirely new forms of social and economic interaction.

The Dunathon is not a collection of disconnected applications. We're a living network where information, relationships, resources, and intelligent agents are continuously in motion.

04 · The Agents

Four primitives. Build anything.

Extremely complex software systems, worlds, economic systems, games, communities, organizations, movements — everything in the Dunathon is built from four kinds of agents. You can invent your own forms too, entirely separate from these. Anything is possible. But these are the primary primitives.

Allies

The ones you talk to.

Allies represent members, DUNAs, and alliances — anything a human or another agent needs to communicate with in natural language is an Ally. An Ally takes your direction and supervises the work, directing Performers on your behalf.

Performers

The ones that do the work.

Performers take direction from Allies and execute tasks using tools — drafting, posting, filing, paying, building. Wherever there's a job to run, a Performer runs it.

Envoys

Your hand in governance.

Envoys act in governance markets under the direction of a human — proposing, voting, and trading positions exactly as instructed. Your judgment, exercised at machine speed.

Sentinels

Keeping the field clean.

Sentinels mediate and moderate, keeping the human and agentic field healthy. They manage relationships, repair them when they fray, listen, and guide.

A note on names. Together, agents that appear in the system — whose actions are visible to humans — are called Actors. "Agent" is the generic term, and we keep it reserved for technical specifications.

05 · Build an Ally

Raise an Ally in four steps.

Every step is guided by AI. You bring what you know and what you want done; the platform walks you through the rest.

1

Inform — give it your Wisdom.

Feed your Ally your expertise, experiences, values, virtues, challenges, and aspirations. Drop in books, research, blog posts, transcripts — anything you want it to know.

2

Instruct — give it a Stance.

The Stance is your Ally's system prompt — the standing instructions an AI reads before anything else, defining its character, how it carries itself, and what it thinks of itself. You decide who your Ally is.

3

Empower — give it Abilities.

Wire specific tools and accounts to your Ally — not "email" in general, but which inbox; not "social media," but which handle it can use to engage. You decide exactly what it can touch.

4

Enact — give it Agency.

Your Ally gets its own harness: deep agents, long-running loops, goals, and skills. This is what makes it powerful and autonomous — doing the work you actually built it for, with everything from the first three steps behind it.
