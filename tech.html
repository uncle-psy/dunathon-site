The Tech · Agentic AI + Blockchain

Built to be honest.

The Dunathon runs on two technologies that need each other — agentic AI brings the capability, the blockchain makes sure no one can fake what it did.

01 · The two technologies

Agentic AI and the blockchain.

You've heard both terms. Here's what they actually mean, and why each one is incomplete without the other.

The actor

Agentic AI

A regular piece of software waits for you to click something. An agent doesn't. It reads a goal, makes decisions, and acts on them — it books the trip, places the order, sends the message, pays the invoice. You set the direction; it handles the steps.

The catch: on its own, you have to take its word for what it did. There's no independent record. That's a problem when real money and real decisions are involved.

The honest record

The blockchain

A blockchain is a shared ledger — a running record of transactions and decisions — that no single party controls. Every entry is checked by the whole network before it's accepted, and once it's in, no one can change it.

It's not controlled by a company, a government, or a platform. It lives on thousands of independent computers, and they all have to agree before anything is written down.

02 · How it stays honest

A record no one can quietly rig.

"Blockchain" sounds technical. The idea is simple: a way of keeping records no one can quietly change. Three properties make that true.

Decentralized

No one owns it.

The ledger doesn't live on any single server or in any single office. It runs on thousands of independent computers around the world. There's no central office to lean on, bribe, or switch off.

Validated

The whole network agrees.

Before any transaction is accepted, the network has to reach consensus. A lie would have to fool everyone at once — not just one gatekeeper, but thousands of independent participants all checking the same work.

Permanent

The record stays put.

Once something is recorded, it can't be quietly changed or erased. No one gets to cook the books after the fact — not a bad actor, not a platform, not even the people who built the system.

Put them together

A market no one can rig.

Buyers, sellers, and agents play by the same rules in the open, against the same record — on the Solana blockchain. Not a market we promise is fair. A free and fair market by construction.

03 · The platform

You build on a complete platform.

The donation and DUNA technology is a finished stack — the Kinship "Kiduna" platform. You don't assemble anything. Everything a DUNA needs is already built in.

Identity & registration

Your DUNA is real from day one.

Your DUNA is a registered West Virginia entity with a name on the record. Real legal standing, real identity — on file with the Secretary of State. Not a promise; a filing. See the law page for what that means in practice.

Treasury & tokens

Hold value, issue a token, take contributions.

Your DUNA can hold a treasury, issue its own token, and accept contributions — all built in. No custom code, no financial plumbing to set up. The economics page has the full token detail.

Governance & voting

Propose, vote, and pay out on-chain.

Members propose, vote, and the treasury pays out — automatically, on-chain, by the rules your DUNA set. Every decision stays on the record. See law.html for how governance markets work.

You don't need to be technical to run any of this. The platform handles the smart contracts, the token mechanics, and the on-chain records. You handle the mission.

04 · Ready-to-use agents

Start with agents that already work.

No coding knowledge necessary. The Dunathon ships with sample agents you can put to work right away. You configure them in plain language — describe what you want, and the agent does it.

Treasury agent

Tracks the books, drafts the payouts.

Watches the DUNA's treasury in real time, flags anything unusual, and drafts payout proposals for members to review and approve. You stay in control — the agent handles the accounting.

Deadlines & grants agent

Nothing falls through the cracks.

Watches your filing deadlines, compliance calendar, and funding opportunities. Surfaces what's coming before it matters, and drafts alerts so stewards can act on time.

Member-support agent

Answers questions, helps people join.

Handles the everyday stuff — explaining how the DUNA works, helping new members navigate, answering questions about proposals and votes. Frees up your stewards for the work that needs human judgment.

Proof & reporting agent

Gathers evidence, writes it to the record.

Collects the evidence that backs your DUNA's decisions and impact — drafts the report, writes the key facts on-chain. The record is always there; you don't have to maintain it by hand.

Configure in plain language

Not code. Instructions.

You tell an agent what to watch for, what to flag, and how to respond — in plain sentences. The agent handles the rest. If you can write an email, you can configure an agent.

On the record

Every action is accountable.

Agents don't just act — they write what they did to the chain. Members can see what ran, what it decided, and why. That's the blockchain doing its job: keeping the AI honest.
