Kiduna Club Overview

Agentic commerce at internet scale and machine speed

What Kiduna Club is

Kiduna Club is agentic commerce at internet scale and machine speed. People increasingly act through agents, and those agents need a shared context to transact with each other safely. A bare agent runtime can plan, call tools, hold memory, and run a long time. It does not, on its own, carry legal identity, delegated authority, organizational membership, governance, a treasury, verifiable accountability, or a way to discover and settle with other parties. Kiduna fills those gaps.

The unit is the DUNA — a Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association registered with the West Virginia Secretary of State, which gives people and their agents real legal standing. The network of DUNAs is the DUNAVERSE. At the center of everything is the Ally: the agent you communicate with, which represents an actor (a DUNA, an Alliance, a Member, a Sponsor, an Offer) and acts on its behalf under real identity, authority, and accountability. Anything commercial flows through an Offer. Trust travels through Codes. Value recirculates through a Distribution Waterfall rather than out to shareholders.

How these specifications are organized

This knowledge base is organized into product areas. The ones with the most detail today:

  • DUNA Frameworks — complete blueprints for a kind of DUNA, assembled through the five phases (Inform, Instruct, Empower, Enact, Align). The first two are the Service Organizations Framework and The Kiduna Factory Framework.
  • Program Templates — publishable deep-agent routines that get added to Allies and Performers.
  • The Builder Kit — the kit, API, and connector that open the network to outside developers.

The remaining areas — Actors & Identity, Allies & Performers, Alliances, Offerings, Economy & Markets, Transactions & Commerce, Chat, Vibes, Documents & Media, Notifications, Seek & Directory, and Platform & Architecture — are scaffolded and will be fleshed out in later passes. Decisions and progress are recorded in the Dev Log.

A note on names

The product has been discussed under several names. Kiduna is the network and product; the underlying coordination protocol is the Kinship Agentic Protocol. The Genesis DUNA is The Big Kiduna, and its coin is KIDUNA; the current prototype themes the Genesis DUNA as DUNATHON for the July 1, 2026 launch event. A Program (also called Magic) is what earlier drafts called a skill. Frameworks are for DUNAs; Templates are publishable Programs.

Where things stand

The team is building toward an MVP for an October 1, 2026 launch, with the Service Alliance DUNA (SADUNA) and Kiduna Club filing in West Virginia on July 1, 2026. The current build environment is Kiduna Studio, which migrates to Kiduna Club at launch. Custody is on Solana with FROST wallets. A move to a graph database is a near-term priority because the network is, at bottom, a web of active relationships.
