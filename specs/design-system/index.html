Design System · v2
Kiduna · A West Virginia DUNA

Wild, wonderful, and self-governed.

The brand and interface system for Kiduna — a decentralized, member-owned nonprofit rooted in West Virginia. Old Gold on deep navy, a sky-blue counterpoint, and a celestial mark — wild, wonderful, and self-governed.

Clearspace & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the moon on all sides. Never crowd the lockup with type or UI. Minimum legible size: 120px wide on screen, 24mm in print; below that, use the mark alone.

= moon height

Don't

The mark's two-color logic — gold sun, sky moon — is fixed. Protect it.

✕
Don't recolor the mark to orange, magenta, lime, or violet.
✕
Don't stretch, skew, or distort the proportions.
✕
Don't place the color mark on light fields — use the navy mark instead.
✕
Don't add shadows, glows, gradients, or outlines.

06

Voice

Warm, direct, slightly literary, with a civic undertone. We write to members — never "users".

We say

  • members, organizers, stewards, the block, kin
  • register · adopt bylaws · hold a treasury · pass a proposal
  • "A nonprofit, on-chain."

We avoid

  • "users", synergy, leverage, disrupt
  • hollow CTAs — "Learn more", stacked emoji
  • web3 jargon that isn't load-bearing
