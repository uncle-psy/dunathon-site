Kiduna Club Product Specifications

The complete product knowledge base for Kiduna Club — agentic commerce at internet scale and machine speed. Organized into product areas, each linking to its specifications and skills.

This is the top-level hub. Everything the team specifies lives here: the DUNA Frameworks (complete blueprints for a kind of DUNA), the Program Templates (publishable deep-agent routines), the Builder Kit, and the product areas that make up the platform. Pages and skills are interlinked; the skills are Markdown, so they read in the browser and download for an AI agent. Areas marked Planned are scaffolded now and fleshed out later.

New here? Start with DUNA Frameworks, then The Kiduna Factory Framework — the framework for the Genesis DUNA, the first DUNA we are building.
