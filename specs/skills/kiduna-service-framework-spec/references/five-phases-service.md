# Five-phase interview checklist — service organizations

Mark every shipped element **fixed** (inherited unchanged), **fill-in** (the
adopter completes), or **discovered** (the Ally fetches from the network at run
time). Maximize fixed and discovered; minimize fill-in. Enact and Align carry
the most weight in this vertical.

## 1. Inform → Wisdom

- History and lineage; roster and chapters; benefits and resource directories;
  events and traditions; archives (photos, video, documents).
- What is discovered on the network (chapters, members, and history relevant to
  where/when a member served) rather than entered by hand.
- Presentation: timelines, maps, rosters, memorial cards — navigable, linking to
  records, oral-history video, memorial pages. Archive capture to recognized
  standards so it can flow to national repositories.

## 2. Instruct → Stance

- The system prompt: who the Ally is, how it speaks to people who have done hard
  work, what it will and will not do.
- **Bake in the wellbeing guardrails** (see wellbeing-and-safety.md): warm,
  plain-spoken, never poses as a clinician, routes to human help and verified
  crisis lines when distress appears.
- Marked fill-in slots: creed, tone, specific commitments. One Stance per Ally.

## 3. Empower → Abilities

- Typically: email/calendar, community & communications channels, archive/
  document storage, mapping (facilities, historical sites), identity and
  recognition rails.
- Mark sensitive Abilities (health, benefits) for protected handling.
- State which programs depend on which Abilities; a required-but-unconnected
  Ability blocks its program clearly.

## 4. Enact → Programs (the tool suite)

Specify each program adopted from the suite (Roll Call, LegacyLog, Veteran
Voices, Service Connected + Safe Harbor, The Campaign, Wall of Honor, Into the
Breach, The Forge, Recognition Market, Service-Made). For each:

- **Prompt** (exactly one), **Sequence**, **Triggers** (0+), **Database
  Connections** (0+), **Polling/Web Hook** (0+), **Tools/Abilities** (0+).
- **Human-approval points** — anything touching a benefit, a crisis flag, or a
  credential pauses for a human. State them explicitly.
- Long-running/delegation behavior (planning, Performers, resume-after-interrupt).

Service Connected (with Safe Harbor) is the highest-stakes program; spec it
against wellbeing-and-safety.md.

## 5. Align → Sentinel (HEARTS)

- Default HEARTS settings (Harmony, Empowerment, Artistry, Reason, Trust,
  Synthesis), conservative for this population, with adjustable ranges.
- The un-amendable protections (wellbeing, Safe Harbor, child-safety).
- What the Sentinel watches for and when it brings a human in; how its
  interventions are made visible to the humans responsible for the community.

## After the phases

- **Agent-interaction model:** the Avatars (scoped, context-isolated),
  Performers, Electors, and Sentinel the org runs; each agent's purpose; scope
  via Code; human-in-the-loop points; the audit trail; legal attributability.
  Cover member↔org, org↔org, and cross-organization interactions, and the
  concrete workflows (benefits, crisis routing, onboarding/verification,
  heritage/reconnection, governance, recognition/commerce, community
  engagement).
- **Wellbeing / Safe Harbor config:** crisis-line bindings per domain, screeners,
  peer network, clinical ownership.
- **Identity & verification:** domains covered, Verifying Authorities, tier
  policy, issuer multisig.
- **Discovery:** what a member's Ally searches for on the network.
- **Slots:** the consolidated fill-in list for the adopt flow.
- **Composition:** whether the org nests under a parent Duna or joins an Alliance.
