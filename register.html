01 · How it works

Four steps. One genesis DUNA.

The Dunathon is DUNA #1 — the first Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association registered in West Virginia. David Levine registers it as the registered agent for all participants. You don't file anything yourself. You just join.

1

Join the genesis DUNA.

Register once and you're a member of DUNA #1. The Dunathon is your registered agent — no shell company to set up, no paperwork to file yourself, no legal overhead on day one.

2

Start from a shell.

Pick up a ready-made DUNA template and make it yours, or build something entirely new on the same foundation. The scaffold is there. What you do with it is up to you.

3

Build in the open.

This is a collaboration as much as a competition. Share information and resources freely. Every member builds on the one genesis DUNA, so what's good for one tends to be good for all.

4

Vote with tokens.

Members decide what the treasury funds by buying in. You don't need your own DUNA to win — every vote serves the mission and builds the token's value for the whole network.

02 · Why join now

What you get when you show up first.

Joining early isn't about advantage over other members. It's about being part of something while it's still being made.

Meet people

Find your collaborators.

Co-founders, researchers, developers, organizers — the people building the first DUNA economy are all in the same room. Early is when those connections form.

Early access

Get into the tech early.

Registered members get access to the platform and sample agents before the public launch. Learn how DUNAs actually run before the wider world catches up.

Airdrops

Early members receive token airdrops.

As the network comes online, founding members receive token distributions. These aren't promises of returns — they're a record of being present at the start.

A real say

Vote on what we build.

Member votes decide what the treasury funds. Not advisory polls — actual decisions, on-chain, where your participation shapes the direction of the work.

03 · Early access & airdrops

Register before July 1 and you're in early.

A few things available only to founding members.

For members who join at opening

Platform access and token distributions.

Members who register at or near the July 1 opening get early access to the platform and the sample agents — the working tools, not a demo. You can start building before the broader public joins.

As the network comes online through the summer, early members receive token airdrops — distributions to founding participants as a record of their place in the genesis. This isn't an investment instrument and it isn't a promise of financial return. It's a marker: you were here when DUNA #1 was filed. The token's value, if any, comes from the work the network does together.

Registration is free. The Dunathon runs July 7 – September 9, 2026. Anyone can join; anyone can spin out their own DUNA up to the closing date.
04 · Founders, teams, and alliances

Every role is open to you.

The genesis DUNA — DUNA #1 — is the common foundation. David Levine registers it as the registered agent for all participants. From there, your role is yours to define.

Anyone can join

Every DUNA is open.

There are no gatekeepers and no approval process. Register, and you're a member. Every DUNA that forms during the Dunathon is open to members by design.

Become core team

Take on a founder's stake.

If you want more than membership — if you want to build and lead a DUNA — step into a core role. More responsibility, more shaping power, and a founder's stake in what you build.

Be in many · ally

Join more than one.

You can be a member of several DUNAs at once, form alliances between them, and create new DUNAs any time up to September 9. The network is not a tournament bracket.
