04 · How winners are chosen

No judges. Just members.

Winners aren't selected by a panel. They're selected by decision markets — a voting mechanism where you put real support behind what you want to see succeed.

01

A proposal goes live

Each DUNA posts a specific question — a goal it wants to pursue or a commitment it wants to make.

02

Members vote

You cast votes for or against. Votes are backed by tokens — the mechanism explained on the economics page.

03

Support compounds

The more members who join and vote, the more the signal strengthens. It rewards both the mission and the belief that it can be done.

04

The treasury follows

What members approve, the treasury funds. No override, no back-channel deal — the record is open on-chain.

Why this works — a decision market doesn't just measure who likes an idea. It measures who believes it will actually happen. That extra step filters for real commitment, not just applause. Read how the governance system works on the law page and how the economics back it up on the economics page.
