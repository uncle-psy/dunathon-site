Summerlong Dunathon · July 1 – Sept 7, 2026 · Charleston, WV + Online

Now AI agents can act, own, and answer for it.

On July 1, 2026, a new West Virginia law takes effect that gives intelligent agents the three things the open internet never could — identity, authority, and accountability. 01 · The idea

What is agentic commerce?

The short version: software that no longer waits for your clicks — it acts on a goal. When that software starts doing business, you get agentic commerce.

An agent

It acts on its own.

Software that pursues a goal without being walked through each step — it reads, decides, and acts. It books the trip, places the order, makes the call.

Agentic commerce

Agents doing business.

What happens when those agents buy, sell, hire, and pay — with us, and with each other — at the speed of software.

The catch

It's already here.

Agents sign, pay, and transact in production today. The missing piece isn't capability. It's a way to trust the agent on the other side.

02 · What's missing on the open internet

Three things let any business operate in the open. The internet gives agents none of them.

A DUNA — a Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association, registered in West Virginia — supplies all three. Here is each gap, in plain terms, and what closes it.

01 · Identity

Identity

The gap — on the open internet, an agent is anonymous. You can't be sure who, or what, you're dealing with.

The DUNA — a registered organization with a name on file, on the record.

02 · Authority

Authority

The gap — an agent can act, but it can't truly hold anything — own value, or make a deal that sticks.

The DUNA — a real legal entity that can hold a treasury and sign for itself.

03 · Accountability

Accountability

The gap — when something goes wrong online, there's often no one to answer for it.

The DUNA — a recognized entity that answers for what it does, at law.

03 · Why it matters

This isn't the old things, done faster.

Deterministic software made work more efficient. This is something else. We're not here to cut costs or chase enterprise efficiencies.

New possibility spaces

Not solving problems. Opening worlds.

When an agent can hold value and answer for it, it can do things that simply couldn't exist before — fund a cause, run a fair market, steward a commons.

Creativity, facing reality

The world as it is.

We build for how people and agents actually behave — not a tidy diagram of how they should. Creative work, met with honest accounting.

Nonprofit missions

Value flows back to the work.

A DUNA is built around a mission, not owners. It's a place to put resources and information to work for something — and keep it there.

For instance — a nonprofit that runs itself. Members vote to fund the work, the treasury pays it out, and every decision stays on the record. No office. No board. No middleman.

04 · Two technologies

The blockchain keeps the AI honest.

The Dunathon runs on two technologies that need each other. Agentic AI brings the capability. The blockchain makes sure no one can fake what it did.

The actor

Agentic AI

The intelligence that reads, decides, and acts. It's creative, fast, and capable — but on its own, you simply have to take its word for what it did.

The honest record

The blockchain

A shared ledger no single party controls. Every action is checked by the whole network and written down for good. It's how the AI proves what it did.

05 · The legal foundation

The first jurisdiction built for agentic commerce.

On July 1, 2026, the West Virginia DUNA Act takes effect. West Virginia becomes the first — and only — place on Earth where you can register a Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association with the Secretary of State.

Closes the gap

A legal home that didn't exist.

Until now, a crypto project or an AI agent had nowhere to be a real entity — to hold value, sign a deal, and answer for it. West Virginia closes that statutory gap and grants real legal standing.

Cuts fraud & scams

Accountability, on the record.

Anonymous scams plague crypto, and AI makes them faster and cheaper to run. A registered DUNA has its name on the record and answers at law — so fraud has far fewer places to hide.

Read about the law →
06 · Who should govern

Not a platform. Its members.

Every centralized platform answers to its owners first. They set the rules, take a cut, and can change the deal whenever it suits them. A DUNA is governed by the people in it.

The centralized platform

You're the product, not the owner.

The rules, the fees, and the data belong to a company. When its interests and yours diverge, theirs win — and you have little say and nowhere to appeal.

The DUNA

The members set the rules.

Governance happens in the open, on-chain, by member vote. No outside owner to overrule you, no quiet change to the terms. You hold a real say in what gets built and funded.
